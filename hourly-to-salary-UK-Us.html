How hourly pay converts to salary

Hourly-to-salary conversion starts with gross pay: hourly rate multiplied by weekly hours and then by paid working weeks. The result is useful for comparing jobs, but it is not the same as take-home pay. Tax, National Insurance, FICA, state tax, pension contributions, retirement contributions, student loan deductions and benefit deductions can all change the amount that reaches your bank account.

UK and US differences to keep in mind

The hourly conversion formula is simple, but payroll treatment differs by country. UK estimates usually need income tax, National Insurance, pension contributions and student loan plan assumptions. US estimates usually need federal income tax, FICA, state income tax where relevant and retirement contribution assumptions.

Questions about this route

Why does this mixed-case URL exist?

It appears to be an older URL casing for the hourly-to-salary calculator. The current maintained route is lowercase, but keeping this file prevents old crawl requests or saved links from reaching a 404 page.

Is this a separate calculator?

No. For calculations, use the maintained hourly to salary calculator or the US hourly to salary calculator.

Can I use this for take-home pay?

Use it as a routing page only. For take-home pay, continue to UK salary after tax, US salary after tax, or the methodology page to understand how estimates are handled.
