Frequently asked questions

What should this salary feel like after tax?

It should feel very comfortable if fixed costs are managed deliberately, but housing, school fees, travel and lifestyle choices can still absorb the surplus quickly.

Why is the net increase smaller than the gross increase?

Most income in this range is taxed at the additional rate, so a large part of each extra pound is deducted before it reaches take-home pay.

Should pension contributions be considered at this income?

Yes. Pension planning can be important, although annual allowance and taper rules may need professional advice at higher earnings.

Is the monthly figure better for budgeting?

Usually yes. The monthly estimate lines up better with mortgages, rent, bills, childcare, school fees and regular saving plans.

Does this estimate include bonuses?

No. It treats the salary as regular employment income and excludes bonuses, benefits in kind, pension deductions, student loans and tax-code changes.
