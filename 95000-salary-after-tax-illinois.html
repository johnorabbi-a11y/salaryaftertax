Why $95,000 Feels More Predictable in Illinois

It still reduces take-home pay more than Texas or Florida, because Illinois does have state income tax. But the outcome is not usually as compressed as it is in the higher-tax progressive states.

This is why Illinois usually lands in the middle of state-by-state salary comparisons. You do lose some take-home pay to state tax, but the result is often steadier and more predictable than in states where tax pressure ramps up more sharply.
