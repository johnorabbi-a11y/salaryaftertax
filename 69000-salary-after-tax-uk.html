Frequently asked questions

Is this salary enough for a mortgage?

It may support mortgage affordability, but lenders and households will look at commitments, deposit, dependants and regular costs as well as income.

Why does the weekly figure matter?

The weekly figure helps with groceries, travel, fuel and day-to-day spending limits even when the salary is paid monthly.

What deductions are included?

The estimate includes income tax and National Insurance using standard UK assumptions. It does not include pensions, student loans or bonuses.

How should a pay rise be judged?

Judge it by the extra monthly take-home pay, not just the increase in gross salary.

What should I check before relying on this?

Check your tax code, pension contribution, student loan plan and any salary sacrifice arrangement.
