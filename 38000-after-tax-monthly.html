Monthly pay questions

How much is £38,000 after tax per month?

How much is deducted before monthly pay?

Estimated deductions are £593 per month, made up of income tax and employee National Insurance.

Should I budget from gross or net monthly pay?

Use net monthly pay for real budgeting. Gross monthly salary is useful for reference, but it is not the amount available for bills, housing and savings.

Can pension contributions change the monthly figure?

Yes. Pension contributions can reduce current take-home pay, but they may improve long-term savings and can affect taxable income depending on scheme structure.
