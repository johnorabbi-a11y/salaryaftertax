Weekly pay FAQ

How much is £120,000 after tax per week?

The estimate is about £1,465 per week after income tax and employee National Insurance.

Why does £120,000 feel heavily taxed?

Income above £100,000 reduces the personal allowance, which increases the effective tax pressure on part of this salary.

Should I compare the monthly page too?

Yes. The weekly page is best for short-term spending, while the monthly page is better for rent, mortgage, bills and savings planning.

Can pension contributions change weekly take-home pay?

Yes. Pension contributions can reduce immediate net pay and may also affect taxable income, depending on how they are arranged.
