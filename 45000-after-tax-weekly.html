How much is £45,000 after tax per week?

On a £45,000 salary, your gross weekly pay is about £865. After Income Tax and employee National Insurance, weekly take-home pay comes out at roughly £679.

This estimate assumes a normal UK PAYE setup. If you pay into a pension, repay a student loan, receive bonuses, or use salary sacrifice, your weekly net amount may differ from the headline estimate.

Why weekly pay matters

Looking at weekly take-home pay can make a salary easier to understand in real life. It helps when you are comparing offers, working out what overtime is really worth, or seeing how your income stacks up against hourly roles.

For many people, weekly figures also feel more tangible for fuel, food, childcare, and day-to-day household spending.

What can change your weekly take-home pay?

  • Pension deductions: these can reduce weekly take-home but may improve long-term value and tax efficiency.
  • Student loan repayments: these are one of the most common reasons your actual weekly pay comes in below a basic estimate.
  • Bonuses or overtime: extra earnings can move your deductions around from one pay period to the next.
  • Tax code differences: non-standard tax codes can change your net pay noticeably.
  • Workplace deductions: schemes or benefits taken through payroll can also affect what lands in your account.
