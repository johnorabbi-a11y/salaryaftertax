How much is £48,000 after tax per week?

On a £48,000 salary, your gross weekly pay is about £923. After standard UK Income Tax and employee National Insurance deductions, your estimated weekly take-home pay is roughly £717.

This estimate assumes a normal PAYE setup with a standard tax code. If you contribute to a pension, repay a student loan, receive bonuses, or use salary sacrifice schemes, your actual weekly take-home pay can vary.

Why weekly pay matters

Weekly take-home pay makes a salary easier to understand in practical terms. It can help with comparing salaries against hourly jobs, shift work, or overtime-heavy roles, and it often gives a more intuitive picture of day-to-day affordability.

For many households, a weekly figure is useful for budgeting food, fuel, travel, childcare, and other regular expenses.

What can change your weekly take-home pay?

  • Pension deductions: these reduce your weekly take-home pay but can improve long-term retirement value and sometimes tax efficiency.
  • Student loan repayments: these are one of the most common reasons actual weekly pay comes in below a simple estimate.
  • Bonuses and overtime: variable earnings can shift deductions and change the amount you take home in different pay periods.
  • Tax code differences: a non-standard tax code may push weekly deductions higher or lower than expected.
  • Salary sacrifice and workplace benefits: payroll deductions can change taxable income and affect net pay.
