Frequently asked questions

Is this salary comfortable after tax?

It can be workable, but comfort depends heavily on rent, council tax, commuting costs and whether there is another household income.

What should I check before relying on this estimate?

Check your tax code, pension contribution, student loan status and any salary sacrifice, because those can change your payslip.

Is monthly or weekly take-home more useful?

Monthly take-home is usually better for rent, bills and saving plans, while weekly take-home is helpful for day-to-day spending control.

Why compare nearby salaries?

Nearby salaries show how much extra net pay a small raise may create after UK deductions.

Does this include benefits or bonuses?

No. It treats the salary as regular employment income and excludes bonuses, benefits in kind and irregular adjustments.
