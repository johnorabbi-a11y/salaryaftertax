What affects monthly take-home pay?

No state income tax

This is the biggest Florida advantage. More of the gross salary survives into your monthly paycheck because there is no state income tax layer reducing it first.

Housing choice

Florida can feel very different depending on where you live. Housing may be manageable in some areas and much tighter in high-demand coastal or lifestyle-driven markets.

Transport setup

Many areas require regular driving, so fuel, maintenance, insurance, and commute distance can all shape how much of the monthly pay really feels free.

Insurance pressure

This is one of the biggest Florida-specific watchouts. Health, auto, and broader insurance costs can materially change how comfortable the salary feels month to month.

Payroll deductions

401(k) contributions, health insurance premiums, and other payroll deductions can reduce actual monthly take-home even when the tax structure looks favorable.

Debt and savings goals

Childcare, loans, credit cards, and savings targets all determine how far $5,634 per month really goes in practice. The tax efficiency helps, but obligations still define the final lifestyle outcome.
