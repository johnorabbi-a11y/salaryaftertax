How much tax is deducted before monthly pay arrives?

Is this enough for a high-cost UK household budget?

For many households it can support a strong budget, but housing, pension saving, childcare, school fees, commuting and investment goals should be tested against the monthly net figure rather than the gross salary.

Why is the personal allowance not shown?

The standard personal allowance is reduced once income exceeds £100,000 and is fully removed at this level, which increases the effective tax pressure on the salary.
