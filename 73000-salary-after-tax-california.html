California salary feel

California tends to put a squeeze on mid-range salaries. A $73,000 income is clearly workable, but it often feels less roomy than the gross salary suggests because the state tax layer sits on top of federal tax and FICA, and the broader cost backdrop can be demanding.

In lower-cost parts of California, this income can feel reasonably stable. In more expensive areas, especially where rent, transport, and insurance are high, the same salary can tighten quickly.

That makes this a salary that can look solid on paper but feel more modest in practice, especially if you are covering a high housing payment on your own.

Practical comfort level
