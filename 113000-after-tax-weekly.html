Weekly pay questions

How much is this salary after tax each week?

The estimated weekly take-home pay is £1,438. It is a practical guide for short-term budgeting, although your payslip may change if you have pension deductions, student loans, benefits or irregular pay.

Does weekly take-home pay include pension contributions?

Gross pay is the salary before PAYE deductions. The weekly take-home figure is lower because income tax and National Insurance are removed before the money reaches your bank account.

How can I use the weekly number for planning?

Use the weekly number for flexible spending and smaller recurring costs, while using the monthly page for rent, mortgage payments, bills and automated savings. Looking at both views gives a more stable plan.

Is this page the same as the annual salary page?

No. The annual page is better for overall salary assessment, while this page focuses on weekly pay-cycle decisions and short-term budgeting behaviour.

Final summary

On a £113,000 salary, estimated weekly take-home pay is about £1,438 after UK income tax and National Insurance. The figure is most useful when it is treated as a weekly planning limit rather than a direct reflection of gross salary.
