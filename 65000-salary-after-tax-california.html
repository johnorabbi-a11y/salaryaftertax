Why $65,000 After Tax in California Is Different

A California salary page gives a more realistic answer than a generic US take-home estimate. Federal tax and FICA apply everywhere, but California also taxes wage income at state level, which changes what you actually keep from a $65,000 salary.

At this income band, the gap versus no-income-tax states is large enough to matter for budgeting, relocation, and offer comparisons. That makes California an essential page inside a strong US state cluster because it helps readers see how much location changes the final number.
