What affects weekly take-home pay?

Flat Illinois state tax

Illinois uses a flat state income tax, which keeps the deduction structure relatively easy to understand. It still reduces the paycheck more than Texas or Florida, but usually less aggressively than more layered tax states.

Housing and commuting

Your housing cost and transport setup shape how far this weekly paycheck really goes. A sensible rent or mortgage can make this income feel comfortable, while a heavier setup can tighten it quickly.

Seasonal living costs

Heating and broader seasonal utility costs can be a more noticeable part of the budget in Illinois than in warmer states, which affects the practical feel of weekly cash flow.

Payroll deductions

401(k) contributions, health insurance premiums, and other payroll items can reduce the amount that actually lands in your paycheck even if the base salary looks solid.

Pay frequency

Some people are paid weekly, some biweekly, and some semi-monthly. The underlying salary estimate stays the same, but the way the money feels in practice can vary by payroll rhythm.

Debt and family costs

Childcare, student loans, credit cards, and savings targets all change how far $1,246 per week really goes. The balanced tax result helps, but personal commitments still define the real lifestyle outcome.
