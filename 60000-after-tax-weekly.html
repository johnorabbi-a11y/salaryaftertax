How much is £60,000 after tax per week?

On a £60,000 salary, your gross weekly pay is about £1,154. After standard UK Income Tax and employee National Insurance deductions, your estimated weekly take-home pay is roughly £855.

This estimate assumes a normal PAYE setup with a standard tax code. If you contribute to a pension, repay a student loan, receive bonuses, or use salary sacrifice schemes, the amount you actually take home each week can differ.

Why weekly pay matters

Weekly pay can be easier to relate to than a yearly figure. It helps when you compare a salary against hourly roles, shift patterns, or overtime-heavy work, and it can give a clearer feel for weekly spending power.

For many households, a weekly number is useful for budgeting food, fuel, travel, childcare, and other recurring costs.

What can change your weekly take-home pay?

  • Pension deductions: these reduce your weekly take-home pay but can improve long-term retirement value and sometimes tax efficiency.
  • Student loan repayments: these are one of the most common reasons actual weekly pay differs from a simple estimate.
  • Bonuses and overtime: variable earnings can shift deductions and change the amount you take home in different pay periods.
  • Tax code differences: a non-standard tax code may increase or reduce weekly deductions.
  • Salary sacrifice and workplace benefits: payroll deductions can affect taxable income and net pay.
