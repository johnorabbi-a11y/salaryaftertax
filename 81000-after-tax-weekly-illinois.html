What affects weekly take-home pay in Illinois?

This page uses a clean comparison model, but real weekly pay can vary depending on how your salary and payroll deductions are structured. Even two people on the same annual salary may not see the exact same paycheck.

  • 401(k) contributions: pre-tax retirement contributions can reduce taxable income and slightly change federal withholding.
  • Health insurance deductions: payroll deductions for medical, dental, or vision cover can reduce the cash that reaches your bank account.
  • Bonus or overtime income: extra income can be withheld differently and may make some weeks or pay periods look stronger or weaker.
  • Payroll timing: weekly, biweekly, and semimonthly pay schedules can create slight differences in withholding behavior.
  • W-4 selections: your withholding choices affect how much federal tax is taken out across the year.
  • Location within Illinois: while state income tax is flat, the real feel of the weekly paycheck varies a lot depending on housing and commuting costs.
