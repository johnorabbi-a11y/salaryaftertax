UK Tax Bands Explained

UK tax bands determine how much income tax you pay depending on your salary. As income increases, different portions of salary are taxed at different rates.

Why Tax Bands Matter

Understanding tax bands helps explain why salary increases do not always translate directly into take-home pay.

How This Relates to Salary After Tax

Salary after tax pages use these principles to estimate take-home pay across different income levels.
