Is £70,000 a Good Salary in the UK?

A salary of £70,000 per year is considered a very strong salary in the UK, placing you well above the national average and firmly into a high-earning bracket.

After tax, this typically gives you around £4,273 per month, which is a noticeable jump from mid-range salaries and a strong level of monthly take-home pay in most parts of the country.

Is £70,000 Enough to Live Comfortably?

For most people in the UK, £70,000 supports a very comfortable lifestyle. It can allow for:

  • Strong disposable income after bills
  • Meaningful monthly savings
  • Better mortgage affordability
  • More room for childcare, travel, or lifestyle upgrades

That said, how far it goes still depends on:

  • Location, especially London vs the North or Midlands
  • Whether you are a single earner or part of a household income
  • Housing costs and childcare commitments
