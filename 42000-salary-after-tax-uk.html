Frequently asked questions

What does this salary feel like month to month?

The salary can provide a workable balance between essentials and saving, but the feeling depends heavily on housing and travel costs.

Should I focus on annual or monthly pay?

Annual pay is useful for tax context, while monthly pay is more useful for rent, mortgage payments, bills and cash-flow planning.

Does National Insurance make a big difference?

Yes. National Insurance is smaller than income tax here, but it still reduces the amount kept from the headline salary.

How should I judge a pay rise from this salary?

Look at the monthly net increase, because that is the amount available for extra spending, saving or debt repayment.

Does the estimate include student loans?

No. Student loan repayments are not included, so borrowers may see a lower payslip figure.
