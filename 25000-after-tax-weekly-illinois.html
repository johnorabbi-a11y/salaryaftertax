How Strong Is $421 Per Week in Illinois?

A weekly take-home figure of about $421 is still modest, but Illinois usually lands in a steadier middle-ground position than the heavier-taxed states. The flat state income tax means the salary is reduced, but not as sharply as it can be under some more aggressive state tax structures.

This does not make $25,000 feel high, but it does mean the weekly result can feel a little more stable than the equivalent salary in California or New York. Against Texas and Florida, though, Illinois still trails because it does have a state income tax layer.

Why Illinois Weekly Take-Home Sits in the Middle

Illinois is a useful middle-ground state for comparison because it combines a state income tax with a relatively predictable flat-rate structure. That gives it a cleaner result than some progressive-tax states, while still leaving less of the salary in your pocket than the no-tax states.

In practical terms, that means Illinois often avoids being either the strongest or weakest result in the table. It tends to sit in the centre, which is exactly why it should not be treated like a filler page in this cluster.
