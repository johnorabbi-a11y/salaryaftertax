Monthly Deductions Explained

California monthly pay is affected by four main deduction groups. Two of them, Social Security and Medicare, are federal payroll taxes that apply across most states. The other two, federal income tax and California state income tax, create the bigger variation between different salaries and locations.

What reduces monthly pay?

  • Federal income tax withholding
  • California state income tax
  • Social Security payroll tax
  • Medicare payroll tax
  • Optional pre-tax deductions like 401(k) or healthcare

Why this matters monthly

  • Monthly budgeting depends on net pay, not gross salary
  • Housing costs are usually paid monthly
  • Recurring bills and debt repayments sit on monthly cycles
  • California tax drag is easier to feel month by month
  • Even small payroll changes can affect cash flow noticeably
