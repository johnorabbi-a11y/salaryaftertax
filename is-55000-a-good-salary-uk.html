Is £55,000 a Good Salary in the UK?

A salary of £55,000 per year is considered a strong salary in the UK, placing you comfortably above the national average and into a higher earning bracket.

After tax, this typically gives you around £3,540 per month, offering a noticeable step up from mid-range salaries.
