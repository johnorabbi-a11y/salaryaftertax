FAQ: Frequently asked questions

Why does the take-home percentage fall at this salary?

The personal allowance has been fully withdrawn and part of the income is taxed at the additional rate, so the gap between gross and net pay is wider.

Is this salary enough for a large mortgage?

It can support a strong affordability profile, but the practical answer depends on deposit size, debts, dependants, interest rates and the monthly margin you want to keep.

Should pension planning be prioritised?

Yes. Pension contributions, salary sacrifice and allowance rules can materially affect the value of earnings in this band, so tailored advice may be worthwhile.

Why compare nearby salaries?

Nearby salaries show the actual net movement between offers or pay rises after tax and National Insurance, which is often smaller than the gross movement.

What is excluded from this estimate?

It excludes student loans, pension deductions, bonuses, benefits in kind, dividend income and individual tax-code adjustments.
