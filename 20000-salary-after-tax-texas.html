What Does $20,000 Feel Like in Texas?

A $20,000 salary is still a relatively low income in Texas, so this is not a case of “good salary, low tax, problem solved.” It is still tight. Rent, transport, groceries, utilities, and everyday bills can absorb most of the available money fairly quickly, especially for someone living alone or in a more expensive city.

What Texas does offer, though, is a more efficient version of the same salary. Because there is no state income tax, more of the gross pay is preserved. That makes Texas one of the stronger outcomes among the major comparison states, even if the underlying salary itself remains modest.
