Why Monthly Take-Home Pay Looks Better in Texas

The big driver is simple: no Texas state income tax. That means your monthly take-home pay is shaped mainly by federal tax and payroll deductions, rather than an extra state layer cutting into the result.

This is why Texas and Florida tend to cluster together in take-home comparisons. They are not identical in every living-cost situation, but from a pure net-pay perspective they usually preserve more of the gross salary than California, New York, or Illinois.
