FAQ: Frequently asked questions

How does the personal allowance taper affect this salary?

Above £100,000, the personal allowance is gradually withdrawn, which increases the effective deduction rate until the allowance has gone.

Is this a strong salary after tax?

Yes, but the strength depends on housing costs, family commitments, pension contributions and how much of the net monthly pay is already committed.

Can pension contributions change the result?

Yes. Pension contributions can reduce immediate take-home pay and may alter taxable income, so payslip results can differ from this estimate.

Why use monthly and weekly support pages?

The annual page explains deductions, while the monthly and weekly pages help with rent, mortgage, saving rhythm and pay-cycle planning.

Is this calculation exact for every UK employee?

No. It is a planning estimate using UK income tax and National Insurance assumptions, before tax-code, student-loan, pension or benefit adjustments.
