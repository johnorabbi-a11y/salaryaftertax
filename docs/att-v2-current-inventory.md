## Interpretation

The inventory is dominated by salary-number variants, especially annual/monthly/weekly US state pages. The site has a healthy authority/calculator layer, but that layer is drowned by repeated salary routes. If Google has already learned that AfterTaxTool has a high proportion of low-incremental-value URLs, a smaller V2 calculator platform is more coherent than continuing to polish the same footprint.

## Pages Worth Preserving

Preserve the homepage, trust pages, methodology/tax assumptions, broad pay/tax calculators, job-offer and affordability tools, compensation tools, payroll deduction explainers, selected high-income guides, and any existing calculators that solve a distinct input/output problem.

## Pages Worth Repurposing

Repurpose broad salary hubs and selected planning pages into V2 category hubs where the URL still fits user intent. Examples include `salary-after-tax-us.html`, `salary-after-tax-uk.html`, `planning-calculators.html`, broad calculator pages, affordability pages and paycheck/payslip explainers.

## Pages Likely to Be Consolidated or Removed

The largest candidate for removal is the annual/monthly/weekly salary-number inventory. Monthly and weekly pages should become calculator outputs, not separate indexable URLs. State salary ladders belong on SalaryDecoded if rebuilt later, not on AfterTaxTool V2.
