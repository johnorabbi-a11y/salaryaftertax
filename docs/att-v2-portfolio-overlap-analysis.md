# AfterTaxTool V2 Portfolio Overlap Analysis

## SalaryDecoded

SalaryDecoded should own salary libraries, salary-number pages, UK/US salary examples, salary comparisons and cautious programmatic salary expansion. AfterTaxTool should keep broad calculators such as take-home pay, income tax, bonus tax and payroll deduction tools, but should not keep a massive salary-number footprint.

## WorthMyTime

WorthMyTime should own time-value, career tradeoff, job satisfaction, commute-time and work/life decision framing. AfterTaxTool can own the financial arithmetic side: commute cost, job-change break-even, overtime after tax and compensation value.

## CreditRoadmap

CreditRoadmap should own credit readiness, CCJs, defaults, credit utilisation behaviour and borrowing preparation. AfterTaxTool can own repayment arithmetic, affordability, debt-to-income and mortgage payment tools, with restrained contextual links to CreditRoadmap where credit readiness matters.

## Cross-Portfolio Boundary

Each brand should stand alone. Cross-links should be rare, contextual and user-serving. Avoid reciprocal links on every page, shared footer networks or exact-match cross-domain anchor repetition.
