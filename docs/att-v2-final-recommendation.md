# AfterTaxTool V2 Final Recommendation

## Verdict

Yes, the pivot is technically and strategically worth attempting.

The current site is not technically broken. That is the point: if audits are clean and Google still shows little appetite for the wider salary inventory, the controllable issue is probably the inventory proposition itself. AfterTaxTool should stop competing with SalaryDecoded and become a calculator-first money utility.

## Recommended First Implementation Phase

Begin with Phase 0 only: create a URL-level disposition manifest and final V2 calculator shortlist. Do not delete or redirect anything until the manifest exists and the strongest 30-50 V2 calculators are specified.

## What Should Not Happen

- Do not preserve 25,000 URLs for the sake of preserving them.
- Do not redirect thousands of old salary pages to the homepage.
- Do not rebuild another salary platform on AfterTaxTool.
- Do not run repeated sitemap experiments.
- Do not add calculator pages that are only numeric variants.

## The Clean Separation

- SalaryDecoded: salary intelligence and controlled programmatic salary pages.
- AfterTaxTool: distinct calculators for pay, tax and everyday money.
- WorthMyTime: time, work and career-value decisions.
- CreditRoadmap: credit readiness and borrowing preparation.

## Strategic Next Step

Approve a Phase 0 URL-level manifest. Once approved, build the V2 homepage, category hubs and first 30-50 calculators in a clean branch before touching the old salary inventory.
