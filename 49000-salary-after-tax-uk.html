Frequently asked questions

Is the monthly take-home enough for rent or a mortgage?

It depends on location and household setup. The estimate gives a starting point, but housing should be tested against the monthly net figure.

Why is the gross salary higher than the useful amount?

Income tax and National Insurance are deducted before pay reaches your account, so the take-home figure is the practical budget number.

Can salary sacrifice improve the position?

It can in some cases, particularly for pensions or certain benefits, but the exact effect depends on your employer scheme and tax position.

Why use the weekly page as well?

The weekly view is useful for day-to-day spending control, especially where food, travel or family costs are managed weekly.

What assumptions are used?

The estimate uses standard UK income tax and National Insurance assumptions and excludes individual adjustments.
