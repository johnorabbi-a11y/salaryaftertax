Why does $75,000 after tax in California differ from other states?

Compared with Texas and Florida, a $75,000 salary in California usually leaves you with less take-home pay because state income tax sits on top of federal tax and payroll deductions.

Federal tax and payroll tax are the same framework across states for this type of estimate. The main difference comes from whether a state levies income tax, how large that tax is, and whether local taxes or pre-tax deductions change the result.
