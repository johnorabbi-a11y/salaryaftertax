FAQ: Monthly pay questions

How much is £142,000 after tax per month?

The estimate is about £7,463 per month after UK Income Tax and employee National Insurance.

Why is the monthly take-home much lower than gross monthly pay?

Gross monthly pay is about £11,833, but Income Tax and National Insurance are deducted before net pay reaches your account. At this salary level, the tax bands have a large effect.

Does this include pension contributions?

No. Pension contributions, salary sacrifice, student loan deductions, bonuses and workplace benefits can all change the final monthly payslip.

Is the weekly page different from this monthly page?

Yes. The weekly page is better for short-term cash-flow and weekly pay-cycle thinking, while this page focuses on monthly bills, savings and regular household planning.

Should I use this for mortgage affordability?

It is a useful starting point because it shows monthly net pay, but lenders use their own affordability rules and will consider debts, dependants, credit commitments and other income.
