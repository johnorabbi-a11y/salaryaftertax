Average Salary in the UK (2026)

The average salary in the UK sits roughly between £33,000 and £35,000 for full-time workers. However, this figure alone does not tell the full story, as salaries vary widely depending on region, experience, and industry.

Median vs Average Salary

  • Median salary: ~£33,000
  • Mean (average): ~£35,000+

The median is often more useful, as it represents the midpoint of earnings rather than being skewed by very high salaries.

What Is a Good Salary in the UK?

A good salary typically starts around £40,000–£50,000 depending on your location and lifestyle expectations.
