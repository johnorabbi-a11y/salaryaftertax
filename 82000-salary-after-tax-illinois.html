$82,000 Salary After Tax in Illinois (2026)

An $82,000 salary in Illinois sits in a fairly balanced middle ground. It does not get the clean no-state-tax advantage of Texas or Florida, but it also avoids some of the heavier squeeze associated with California or New York. That makes Illinois one of the steadier “midpoint” states for this salary level, where the take-home pay still feels respectable without looking unusually inflated.

The main reason Illinois lands in that middle zone is its flat state income tax. Federal tax, Social Security, Medicare, and Illinois state tax all reduce the gross salary before it reaches you, but the overall result is still noticeably cleaner than some higher-pressure state comparisons. In practical terms, that means $82,000 in Illinois can often feel solid and stable, though not especially loose if housing or other fixed costs run high.
