Is $70,000 a Good Salary in the US?

A $70,000 salary in the US is generally considered a solid mid-range income, but whether it is “good” depends on location, lifestyle and expenses.

After tax, $70k provides a comfortable income in many parts of the country, but may feel tighter in high-cost areas like California or New York.

Where $70,000 usually feels strong or stretched

A $70,000 salary is often enough to support steady rent, regular bills, retirement contributions and some savings when housing costs are moderate. It is less reliable as a comfort marker in expensive metro areas where rent, insurance, commuting and childcare can absorb the apparent surplus quickly.

The practical test is monthly resilience. If the after-tax income leaves room after housing, transport, healthcare premiums, debt payments and emergency savings, $70k can feel genuinely stable. If those fixed costs are high, the same salary may still require careful tradeoffs.
