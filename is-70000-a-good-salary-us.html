Is $70,000 a Good Salary in the US?

A $70,000 salary in the US is generally considered a solid mid-range income, but whether it is “good” depends on location, lifestyle and expenses.

After tax, $70k provides a comfortable income in many parts of the country, but may feel tighter in high-cost areas like California or New York.
