£20 an Hour Salary UK

Earning £20 an hour in the UK works out to roughly £41,600 a year before tax if you work a standard 40-hour week. This guide shows the annual, monthly and weekly equivalents, explains what affects your take-home pay, and links you to related salary pages and calculators.

Annual salary £41,600
Monthly pay £3,466
Weekly pay £800
Quick answer: £20 an hour is about £41,600 per year, £3,466 per month and £800 per week before tax on a 40-hour working week.

£20 an hour salary conversions

The exact yearly salary from £20 per hour depends on how many hours you work each week. These are the most common UK full-time examples.

Hours per week Weekly pay Monthly pay Annual salary
40 hours £800 £3,466 £41,600
37.5 hours £750 £3,250 £39,000
35 hours £700 £3,033 £36,400

These figures assume 52 paid weeks per year and show gross pay before tax and deductions.

What is the take-home pay on £20 an hour?

Your actual take-home pay will be lower than your gross salary because of income tax and National Insurance. It can also change depending on:

  • your tax code
  • pension contributions
  • student loan repayments
  • overtime or bonuses
  • whether you work the same hours every week

How the calculation works

  • Weekly pay = hourly rate × hours worked
  • Annual pay = weekly pay × weeks per year
  • Monthly pay = annual salary ÷ 12

Example calculation

For a 40-hour week:

  • £20 × 40 = £800 per week
  • £800 × 52 = £41,600 per year
  • £41,600 ÷ 12 = about £3,466 per month

Is £20 an hour a good wage in the UK?

Whether £20 an hour is a good wage depends on your location, housing costs, household size and deductions. In many parts of the UK, it is above minimum wage and translates into a full-time gross annual salary around the low- to mid-£40k range on a 40-hour week.
