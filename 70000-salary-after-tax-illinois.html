Why Illinois take-home pay is lower

Compared with states such as Texas and Florida, Illinois usually leaves you with lower net pay because state income tax applies on top of federal tax and FICA payroll deductions.

  • Federal tax still takes a sizeable share of your salary.
  • FICA taxes still apply through Social Security and Medicare.
  • Illinois state tax is the main extra cost compared with no-tax states.
  • Your real take-home can still move depending on benefits, retirement contributions, and filing status.

What can change your $70k take-home?

  • Filing status such as single, married filing jointly, or head of household
  • Pre-tax deductions like 401(k), medical insurance, FSA, or HSA
  • Bonus income, overtime, or uneven pay timing
  • Extra withholding choices on your W-4
  • Employer-specific deductions and payroll treatment
