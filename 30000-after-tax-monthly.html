How £30,000 Monthly Pay Works

On a £30,000 salary, your income is taxed at the basic UK rate after the personal allowance. National Insurance is also deducted from earnings above the threshold.

At this level, deductions are still relatively low compared to higher salary bands, meaning you keep a large portion of your income each month.
