What can change the monthly feel?

The monthly figure is best treated as a planning number, not a promise of comfort. A household with a modest mortgage, predictable bills and short commuting costs may experience this salary as genuinely roomy, while a household with high rent, nursery fees or expensive rail travel may find that much of the comfort margin disappears before discretionary spending begins.

It is also worth separating recurring costs from occasional costs. Holidays, insurance renewals, car maintenance, professional fees and family commitments can make a strong monthly salary feel uneven if they are not planned for throughout the year.

For that reason, a good monthly budget usually gives every pound a job before lifestyle upgrades are considered: essential bills first, pension and savings second, flexible spending last.
