Overtime Tax Explained

Overtime is often said to be ?taxed more?, but the reality is more subtle. Overtime can push a paycheck into higher withholding for that pay period, yet the final tax result depends on total annual income and marginal tax rules.

The overtime taxed more myth

Overtime pay is usually taxed as ordinary income. The reason it can feel taxed more is that payroll systems may withhold based on the larger paycheck, and extra earnings may fall into a higher marginal band for part of the year.

Marginal tax in plain English

A higher marginal rate usually applies to the next slice of income, not the entire paycheck. Working overtime can increase the taxable income in that period, but it does not make all regular earnings suddenly taxed at the highest rate.

PAYE, FICA and paycheck variability

In the UK, overtime can affect PAYE and National Insurance in the pay period. In the US, overtime can increase federal withholding, FICA and state withholding. The paycheck effect can be real even when the annual tax story is less dramatic.

Shift-worker planning

Overtime can be valuable, but the decision is not only financial. Transport, childcare, fatigue, lost time and inconsistent scheduling all affect whether extra hours are worth the net pay.

Questions this page helps answer

Why can compensation feel different from the headline amount?

Tax, withholding, payroll timing, benefits, deductions and income variability can all change the money that actually reaches the bank account.

Should I use this instead of a calculator?

No. Use it with the calculators and salary pages. The guide explains why the result may differ from a simple salary conversion.

Does this apply to both UK and US users?

Some concepts are country-specific, but the main planning idea is shared: judge compensation by realistic take-home pay and timing.

When should I get professional help?

Complex equity, contractor income, tax residency, payroll disputes or self-employed situations are good reasons to check records and speak with a qualified professional.
