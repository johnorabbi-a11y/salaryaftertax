What Affects Weekly Take-Home Pay?

No Texas state tax

This is the main reason your weekly take-home is stronger here than in California or New York.

Federal deductions

Federal tax still takes the biggest overall share and increases as taxable income rises.

Payroll taxes

Social Security and Medicare reduce every paycheck consistently, even in a no-state-tax state.

Retirement contributions

Pre-tax retirement savings can reduce federal taxable income, though FICA generally still applies.

Benefits and insurance

Health insurance and other employer deductions may reduce your actual weekly banked amount beyond this estimate.

Pay schedule

Some people are paid weekly, some biweekly, and some semimonthly. The weekly figure helps level those differences out.
