£31,000 After Tax UK

A £31,000 salary in the UK is estimated to leave about £25,840 per year after tax. That is roughly £2,153 per month or £497 per week after Income Tax and National Insurance.

The annual figure is close to many mainstream UK roles, so monthly and weekly take-home pay are the numbers most people actually plan around.

It can feel comfortable in lower-cost areas and tighter in expensive cities, especially once rent and travel are included.
