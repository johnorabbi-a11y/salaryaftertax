Frequently asked questions

Is this a higher-rate salary?

Yes. A meaningful share of this income sits above the basic-rate band, so gross increases should be judged by their net effect.

What is the best planning number?

The monthly take-home estimate is usually the most useful number for mortgages, childcare, savings and recurring commitments.

Should pension planning matter here?

Yes. Pension contributions can be a major part of planning at this level, although personal allowance and allowance rules may need advice.

Does the estimate include student loans?

No. Student loans are not included, so borrowers may see a lower net payslip.

Why compare nearby salaries?

Nearby salaries help show whether a promotion, counter-offer or job move creates enough net benefit to justify the change.
