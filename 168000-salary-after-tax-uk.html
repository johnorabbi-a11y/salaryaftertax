How to use this estimate

Treat this as a clean baseline for salary comparison. It is most useful before adjusting for pension contributions, employer benefits, bonus arrangements or advice specific to your household.

For high earners, it is also worth reviewing pension allowance rules and whether taxable income can be managed more efficiently.

FAQ: Frequently asked questions

Why is the net pay so much lower than the gross salary?

The estimate reflects UK income tax and National Insurance at a level where the personal allowance has been withdrawn and additional-rate tax can apply.

Is this salary enough for a high mortgage?

It may support a strong borrowing position, but affordability still depends on deposit size, existing debts, interest rates and how much monthly flexibility you want to keep.

Should pension contributions be reviewed?

Yes. Pension planning can be especially important in this band because tax efficiency, annual allowance rules and employer schemes can materially change the outcome.

Why compare nearby salaries?

Nearby comparisons show the real net difference between pay offers, promotions and retention packages after deductions.

What is not included in this estimate?

It excludes student loans, pension salary sacrifice, bonuses, benefits in kind, investment income and any personalised tax-code changes.
