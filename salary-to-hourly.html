How salary converts to hourly pay

To convert salary into hourly pay, divide annual salary by the total hours worked in a year.

A common full-time assumption is: 40 hours per week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours per year.

So if you earn $50,000 or £50,000 per year and work 40 hours a week, your gross hourly rate is about 24.04 per hour.

This calculator is best used as a quick comparison tool. Your actual effective hourly earnings can change if you work unpaid overtime, have bonuses, or work fewer than 52 weeks per year.

Frequently asked questions

How do you convert salary to hourly wage?

Divide annual salary by total hours worked in a year. For a 40-hour week over 52 weeks, divide salary by 2,080.

What is a 50,000 salary per hour?

A 50,000 salary works out to about 24.04 per hour before tax if you work 40 hours a week for 52 weeks.

Does this calculator show after-tax pay?

No. This page gives a gross salary-to-hourly conversion. For take-home pay estimates, use the salary after tax pages linked above.

Can I use this for UK and US salaries?

Yes. The calculator works as a simple gross-pay conversion for both UK and US salary examples. Tax treatment differs by country, so use the relevant after-tax pages for net pay estimates.
