Why are deductions so high?

At this income level the personal allowance has gone and part of the salary is taxed at additional-rate levels, so each extra pound has a smaller net effect.

Is weekly budgeting still useful for high earners?

Yes. Weekly budgeting helps separate routine spending from pension planning, investments, tax reserves and large annual commitments.
