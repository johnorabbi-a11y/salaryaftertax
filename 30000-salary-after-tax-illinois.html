How $30,000 Feels in Illinois

A true middle-ground result

Illinois is not as clean as Texas or Florida because there is still state income tax, but it also does not usually feel as punishing as California or New York in this lower salary band. That is why Illinois tends to sit squarely in the middle of the comparison.

At $30,000, that middle position matters. It means the salary still feels tight, but not quite as squeezed as the heavier-tax states.

Still a modest income overall

Even in a middle-ground tax state, $30,000 remains a modest salary. Housing, transport, utilities, groceries, and day-to-day spending can still take up most of the monthly budget.

So Illinois should not be treated as filler here. It has its own identity: not a no-tax winner, not a heavy-tax loser, but a practical middle result.
