How this estimate is built

1. Federal baseline

The estimate starts with a single-filer federal setup using a standard deduction of roughly $16,100 for 2026. That reduces taxable income before the federal brackets are applied.

2. FICA payroll taxes

Social Security is estimated at 6.2% and Medicare at 1.45%. These payroll deductions apply regardless of Texas having no state income tax.

3. No Texas income tax

Texas does not add state income tax to wages. That keeps the overall tax drag cleaner and makes net pay more efficient than in many other large states.
