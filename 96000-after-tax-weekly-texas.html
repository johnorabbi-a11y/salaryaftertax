What affects weekly take-home pay?

The estimate on this page is a useful baseline, but actual weekly pay can vary depending on your payroll and benefit setup. Even people on the same salary may not see the exact same weekly net amount.

  • 401(k) contributions: reduce taxable income and change what lands in your check.
  • Health insurance deductions: can take a noticeable bite out of weekly pay.
  • Pay frequency: weekly, biweekly, and semi-monthly schedules feel different in practice.
  • Bonuses or overtime: may be withheld differently and temporarily distort take-home pay.
  • Benefit elections: HSA, FSA, and other payroll deductions all matter.
  • Housing and car costs: the same weekly paycheck feels very different depending on these big categories.
