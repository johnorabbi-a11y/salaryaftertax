What affects take-home pay on a $78,000 salary?

Even in a no-state-tax state like Texas, two people earning the same salary may not take home exactly the same amount. Payroll deductions and personal setup still matter.

  • Filing status: tax withholding changes depending on household situation.
  • 401(k) contributions: pre-tax retirement saving can reduce taxable income.
  • Health insurance premiums: employer deductions reduce take-home but may improve benefits value.
  • HSA or FSA contributions: these can lower taxable pay and shift net income.
  • Bonuses and overtime: supplemental pay can be withheld differently from base salary.
  • Local living costs: not a tax issue, but crucial to how far the salary really stretches.
