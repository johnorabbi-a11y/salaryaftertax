Frequently asked questions

Is this a comfortable UK salary after tax?

It can be comfortable for many single-adult budgets, but rent, commuting costs, council tax and household size can quickly change the result.

Which figure should I use for planning?

The monthly take-home estimate is usually the best planning number because most bills, rent and saving goals are monthly.

Can pension contributions change the take-home pay?

Yes. Pension contributions and salary sacrifice can reduce taxable pay and alter the amount that appears on your payslip.

Why compare nearby salaries?

Nearby salaries show how much of a pay rise is actually retained after UK income tax and National Insurance.

Are these figures exact?

No. They are rounded planning estimates and do not include student loans, unusual tax codes, bonuses or benefits in kind.
