The estimated weekly take-home pay is £1,429. It gives a realistic short-term planning figure, although pension contributions, student loans, benefits and payroll timing may change the payslip.

Does the estimate include pension deductions?

Gross pay is before PAYE deductions. The weekly take-home amount is lower because income tax and National Insurance are removed before the money reaches your account.
