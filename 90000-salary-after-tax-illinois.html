Is $90,000 a Good Salary in Illinois?

Yes, $90,000 is a good salary in Illinois. It offers a solid take-home result and lands in a useful middle ground between the strongest no-tax states and the weaker higher-tax states. Because Illinois uses a flat income tax, the outcome is relatively straightforward and predictable.

For many people, this income can support a comfortable lifestyle with room for savings, especially outside the highest-cost pockets. It may not feel as efficient as Texas or Florida, but it usually feels less squeezed than California or New York.
