Why Illinois Feels Different at $85k

In Illinois, $85,000 is a solid income, but the flat state tax means you do not get the no-tax advantage that workers in Texas or Florida enjoy. At the same time, Illinois usually feels more straightforward than states with heavier progressive state tax systems.

The main reasons this salary feels different in Illinois are:

  • Federal tax still takes the biggest share
  • Illinois flat state tax adds a steady extra deduction
  • FICA payroll taxes apply on top
  • Cost of living can vary a lot depending on where you live in the state

That means $85k in Illinois often feels like a stable middle-to-upper income salary, but not as efficient as the same pay in no-state-tax states.
