What affects weekly take-home pay in Florida?

The strongest factor in Florida is the simple tax structure. There is no state income tax on wages, so the main deductions are federal income tax, Social Security, and Medicare. That allows the weekly result to come through more clearly and gives the paycheck a cleaner feel.

But weekly life is still influenced by more than tax. Housing costs can vary a lot from one part of Florida to another, and insurance can be a meaningful expense. Those two areas are often the main reasons a strong weekly paycheck can still feel more stretched in some setups than in others.

Even with that caveat, Florida remains one of the stronger states for this income level. The weekly paycheck is efficient, easy to understand, and usually offers more breathing room than the same gross salary would produce in states with layered wage taxes.
