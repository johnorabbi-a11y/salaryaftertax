Is £45,000 a Good Salary in the UK?

A salary of £45,000 per year in the UK is considered a solid mid-range income, offering a comfortable lifestyle in most regions outside of London.

💰 Monthly Take-Home Pay

After Income Tax and National Insurance, a £45,000 salary typically results in:

  • £2,850–£2,950 per month take-home pay
  • ~£34,000–£35,500 per year after tax

🏡 What Lifestyle Does £45k Support?

  • Comfortable living outside major cities
  • Ability to save monthly (£300–£800 typical)
  • Moderate discretionary spending
  • Suitable for single earners or dual-income households

📊 Comparison to UK Average

The UK average salary sits around £33,000–£35,000, meaning £45,000 is:

  • Above average nationally
  • Upper-middle income in many regions
  • Lower-middle in London

📈 Progression Potential

£45k is often a stepping stone salary, with progression toward:

  • £50,000 salary
  • £55,000 salary
