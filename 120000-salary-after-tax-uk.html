Why is tax so high on £120,000?

At this level of income, your Personal Allowance is heavily reduced because earnings are above £100,000. That means more of your salary is taxed, so the increase in take-home pay is smaller than many people expect.

Key reason this salary feels heavily taxed

  • the Personal Allowance is tapered away once income exceeds £100,000
  • more of your salary falls into the higher-rate band
  • National Insurance still applies on qualifying earnings

What could change your real take-home pay?

Your actual payslip may differ from this estimate depending on your circumstances. Common reasons include:

  • pension contributions
  • student loan repayments
  • bonus payments
  • salary sacrifice arrangements
  • company benefits
  • a different tax code
